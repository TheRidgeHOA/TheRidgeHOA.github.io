Annual Dues & Fees

The figures below summarize the assessments and fees that apply to lots in The Ridge-Heath. They are drawn from the Association's recorded governing documents.

  • Annual Dues

    {{ fees.annual_dues_amount }} per {{ fees.annual_dues_per }}

    Annual dues are due {{ fees.dues_due_date }}. An account is considered delinquent if dues are not paid by {{ fees.dues_delinquent_date }}.

  • Administrative Fee on Delinquent Accounts

    {{ fees.admin_fee_amount }} per {{ fees.admin_fee_per }}

    A {{ fees.admin_fee_amount }} administrative fee is applied on the first of each month an account remains delinquent. This fee reflects the administrative costs the Association incurs in attempting to collect past-due amounts.

  • Ownership Transfer Fee

    {{ fees.transfer_fee_amount }}

    A one-time fee charged when ownership of a lot is transferred.

Non-Payment, Liens & Covenant Enforcement

Two separate documents apply here: one covers liens and remedies for unpaid dues and assessments, while the other covers fines for covenant violations. Each card below opens the relevant document in a new tab.
