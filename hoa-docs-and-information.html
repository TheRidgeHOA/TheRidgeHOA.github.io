Official HOA Documents

The official governing documents and forms for The Ridge-Heath are available below. Each document is offered in two formats.

The reading copy is a clean, searchable re-typeset version generated from the scanned original purely for readability - it is easier to read on screen and can be searched and copied from. The original scan is the document as it was recorded; that recorded original remains the authoritative version. Where the two differ, the original recorded document controls.

Every link opens in a new tab. If you need additional information or have questions regarding these documents, please contact your HOA representative.
