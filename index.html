Community Overview

Located about 20 miles east of Dallas near Lake Ray Hubbard, Heath, Texas is a unique, friendly, and family-oriented community in south Rockwall County. No neighborhood represents the country atmosphere of Heath quite like “The Ridge” – a community of 51 homes on 53 large estate lots.

Community & Amenities

A signature feature of The Ridge is the HOA-owned Common Recreational Area – a shared natural amenity that gives the neighborhood its open, country character.

45-Acre Common Recreational Area

The HOA owns a 45-acre Common Recreational Area (CRA) shared by residents – ample space for gatherings, fishing, and recreation.

4+ Acre Lake & Dock

Within the CRA, a lake of more than four acres with a dock offers a peaceful setting for fishing and enjoying the water.
